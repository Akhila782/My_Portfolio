About Me

Clinical Informatics Analyst with over three years of hands-on experience optimizing EHR/EMR systems, streamlining clinical workflows, and supporting multi-facility clinical application implementations. Skilled in HIPAA compliance, workflow analysis, system configuration, and end-user training.

My Professional Approach

Who I Am

I am dedicated to bridging the gap between healthcare technology and clinical practice. I focus on optimizing EHR environments to reduce clinician burnout, ensure strict patient data privacy, and ultimately improve the quality of patient care.

What I Do
